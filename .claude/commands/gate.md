---
description: Review and sign off a phase gate
argument-hint: <phase number>
---

Prepare the gate review for phase **$ARGUMENTS**.

Present, concisely:
- What was built, and what was deliberately not built.
- Every verification step run, with its result.
- Defects found, fixed, and still open with severity.
- Residual risks and what they mean operationally.
- Anything that needs the owner's decision before the next phase.

Then state your recommendation: PASS, PASS WITH CONDITIONS (list them), or FAIL.

Do not write the sign-off yourself. Wait for the owner.
